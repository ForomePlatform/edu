# Forome Educational Materials

Open educational materials and tools from the [Forome Association](https://forome.org).

## Contents

| Directory | Description |
|---|---|
| [`EuclideanGeometry/`](EuclideanGeometry/) | Catalogs of an ontology for annotating Euclidean geometry problems and their solutions — geometric **facts**, **objects**, and **methods** — in English and Russian, with translation tables and a worked solution-graph example. |

The Euclidean geometry ontology was developed in 1991–1996 and has been used to
annotate thousands of geometry problems in support of curriculum design and
skill tracking. It is documented in the article:

> M. Bouzinier, S. Trifonov, M. Chen, T. Venkatesh, L. Rifkin.
> *An Ontology-Based Approach to Optimizing Geometry Problem Sets for Skill
> Development.* [arXiv:2509.02758](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.02758)
> (submitted to the International Journal of Mathematical Education in Science
> and Technology).
